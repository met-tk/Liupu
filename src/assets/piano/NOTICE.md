# Piano sample attribution

Salamander Grand Piano by **Alexander Holm**, recorded from a **Yamaha C5**.
Audio licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

This project includes three velocity layers (3, 8, 13), 30 pitches per layer,
covering A0–C8. The 90 stereo MP3 files are unmodified copies from
[@tonejs/piano](https://github.com/tambien/Piano/tree/0cd2c034f820c53e83ab22f5c13bd490b9e4de85/audio).
